The plant story

Purwoceng grows in the cool mountains of Java, including the Dieng Plateau in Central Java, at roughly 1,800 to 3,000 metres above sea level. It is an aromatic plant of the carrot family, and the root is the part most used.

For generations, people in the Dieng highlands have used it in traditional herbal preparations. Powdered root is still sold there and brewed like coffee.

Wild purwoceng has become rare, and it is listed as endangered on the IUCN Red List. Responsible sourcing matters to us, and we are confirming with our supplier whether the raw material is cultivated.

Look for the botanical name Pimpinella pruatjan (sometimes written Pimpinella alpina) on the label. Ours is made from the root of that plant.

Traditional use

Traditionally used in Javanese herbal practice to support male vitality and general well-being.

Traditionally the root is prepared in hot water. Water is the only solvent used to make our extract.

Questions people ask

What is purwoceng, and is it the same as Pimpinella alpina?

Purwoceng is the Indonesian name for Pimpinella pruatjan, a plant of the carrot family from the highlands of Java. Researchers also write its name as Pimpinella alpina, and Pruatjan is another spelling for the same plant.

What is it traditionally used for?

Traditionally used in Javanese herbal practice to support male vitality and general well-being. We make no claims about hormones, testosterone, libido or sexual function.

Is it an aphrodisiac?

Traditional and scientific sources describe several uses for purwoceng. We make no claims about sexual function or hormones. If this is your reason for considering it, please speak to a doctor.

How long until I notice anything?

Everyone is different, and we make no promises about results. Traditional herbs are part of a routine, so follow the directions consistently.

Has it been studied for safety?

Yes, in animals. In a 28-day laboratory study in rats, an ethanol extract of the root was reported to cause liver changes at one dose and kidney changes at a higher dose. In a 90-day study, the extract raised kidney markers and caused liver cell changes at the doses tested, and the researchers could not establish a safe dose for chronic use. These were animal studies on ethanol extracts, which differ from our water extract, and they do not show what happens in people. No safe long-term human dose has been established. If you have a liver or kidney condition, please do not use it without speaking to a healthcare professional. The sources are listed at the end of this page.

Is it suitable for women?

Our product is not for use under 18, or by pregnant or nursing women. A laboratory study in pregnant rats reported earlier reproductive development in the offspring. For anyone else, please speak to a healthcare professional. We do not give separate dosing for women.

Can I take it with my medication?

Speak to your doctor or pharmacist first. We do not hold interaction data for purwoceng.

When and how should I take it?

Take 1 capsule 1 to 2 hours before breakfast and before lunch, on an empty stomach, Monday to Friday, with weekends as rest days. Do not exceed 2 capsules in 24 hours.

How long can I keep taking it?

Follow the Monday to Friday routine, with weekends off. Because long-term safety has not been established, please do not use it continuously for long periods without speaking to a healthcare professional.

Is purwoceng endangered?

Wild purwoceng has become rare, and it is listed as endangered on the IUCN Red List. Responsible sourcing matters to us, and we are confirming with our supplier whether the raw material is cultivated.

How do I know it is real purwoceng?

Indonesian researchers stress that confirming the identity of the plant matters, because purwoceng can be substituted with other plants that have similar roots. Check for the botanical name Pimpinella pruatjan, and ask any brand for its Certificate of Analysis. Ours records Pimpinella pruatjan root, and our raw-material results are on this page.

It is an extraction ratio: roughly ten parts of dried root go into each part of finished extract.
