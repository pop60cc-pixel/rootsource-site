What it is

Menthol is the cooling compound found in mint oils. As crystals it is very potent, which is why a tiny amount goes a long way, and why it must be diluted before it goes anywhere near skin.

Traditional use

Traditionally used to support clear breathing and a cooling sensation.

We make no claims about pain, congestion, colds or sinus problems.

How to use it

On skin: always diluted, never directly.

  1. For skin use, dissolve the crystals in a carrier oil or unscented lotion first, at no more than 1g of crystals per 100g, and start with less.
  2. To dissolve: gently warm the carrier oil in a heat-safe container, add the crystals, stir until fully dissolved, and let it cool.
  3. For a lotion, dissolve the crystals in a little carrier oil first, then blend that into the lotion.
  4. Patch test first: apply a little of the diluted mixture to your inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Do not use it if redness, itching or burning appears.
  5. Do not use on broken or irritated skin, or near the eyes, nostrils or mucous membranes. Wear gloves when handling the crystals and wash your hands afterwards.

Steam aroma (use extreme caution)

  • Use hot water that is not boiling, such as hot tap water or boiled water that has stood for a few minutes. Add 1 or 2 crystals to a bowl on a stable surface, never on your lap. The crystals melt at 41 to 44°C and release their aroma with the steam.
  • Keep your face at a safe distance, close your eyes, and breathe gently for 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Stop if you feel dizzy, cough, or have difficulty breathing.
  • Ask a doctor before inhaling if you have asthma, emphysema or a persistent cough.
  • Do not use steam with children under 6. For older children, an adult must stay with them throughout.

Who should be careful

  • Not for children under 6.
  • Not for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Do not use if you are allergic to menthol or peppermint.
  • Do not swallow. External use and inhalation only.
  • If you have sensitive skin, consult a healthcare professional before use.

Questions people ask

Can I put menthol crystals directly on my skin?

No. Menthol is a strong irritant in concentrated form. Always dissolve it in a carrier oil or an unscented lotion first, and do a 24-hour patch test on your inner forearm before using it more widely.

How much should I use?

No more than 1g of crystals per 100g of carrier oil or lotion, and start with less. More is not better, and a stronger mixture raises the risk of irritation.

Why won't it dissolve in water?

Menthol is only very slightly soluble in water, but it dissolves readily in oils and alcohol. In hot water the crystals melt, because they melt at 41 to 44°C, but they do not make an even solution. For that reason, water is not suitable for making a skin mixture. Use a carrier oil.

How do I make a diluted mixture?

Gently warm a carrier oil in a heat-safe container, add the crystals, stir until fully dissolved, and let it cool. For a lotion, dissolve the crystals in a little oil first, then blend that into the lotion.

How do I use it for steam?

Use hot water that is not boiling, such as hot tap water or boiled water that has stood for a few minutes, in a bowl on a stable surface. Add 1 or 2 crystals, keep your face at a safe distance, close your eyes, and breathe gently for 5 to 10 minutes. Stop if you feel dizzy, cough, or have difficulty breathing. Hot water can scald, so take extreme care.

Is steam inhalation safe?

No method of steam inhalation is risk-free. Hot water and steam can scald, and burns happen most often in young children. In 2025 the National Health Service and the British Burn Association urged people to avoid steam inhalation from bowls of hot water for coughs and colds, after a rise in burns, and said there is no strong evidence that it helps. We make no claims about congestion. If you choose to use steam for its aroma, use hot water that is not boiling, keep the bowl on a stable surface, never on your lap, take extreme care around children, and stop if you feel any discomfort.

Can children use it?

Our menthol is not for children under 6, and steam should not be used with them. For older children, an adult must stay with them throughout, because hot water and steam can scald. Keep menthol away from the faces of infants and young children.

What side effects can it cause?

Mild skin irritation, a tingling or burning feeling, and watery eyes can happen, especially when the mixture is too strong. If you notice redness, itching or burning, wash the area and stop using it.

What if I get it in my eyes?

Rinse with plenty of clean water and seek medical advice if discomfort continues. Keep menthol away from your eyes, nostrils and mouth.

What if someone swallows it?

Do not swallow menthol. If it is swallowed, get medical help or contact a poison information centre straight away.

Is it safe if I have asthma?

Ask a doctor before inhaling menthol if you have asthma, emphysema or a persistent cough. Stop at once if you have any trouble breathing.

Is it safe in pregnancy?

No. Please do not use it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

How should I store it?

Keep the jar tightly sealed, in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Menthol crystals can evaporate if the container is not sealed, and they lose strength. The shelf life is 24 months. Keep it away from children and pets.
